Quick pattern: set up the categories

Fills every slide from the starting slide to the last slide, repeating this category layout until the slides run out (so five categories of the same shape are one click). Slides before the start, like your home board, are left alone. "Goes back to the home slide" uses the Home slide chosen above. Every slide can still be changed by hand afterwards.
