🎡 Spin the Wheel

Pulls my live Letterboxd watchlist, spins for a random movie, and shows where to watch it.

How it works

  1. Hit Refresh Watchlist on the wheel page to pull whatever's currently on my Letterboxd watchlist.
  2. Optionally filter by genre or max runtime before spinning.
  3. Spin the wheel — it lands on a film and shows its poster, year, runtime and where to stream it.
  4. Spin again as many times as you like. To take a film off the wheel, just remove it from my Letterboxd watchlist and refresh.
